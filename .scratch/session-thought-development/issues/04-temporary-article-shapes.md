# 04 — Explore and revise temporary Article Shapes

**What to build:** Let a Writer explicitly compare and adapt up to three temporary ways of organizing their own Session Notes, without receiving generated Article prose.

**Blocked by:** 02 — Collect Session Notes and show Development Readiness.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Explore structures becomes available only after a central-point note and at least two supporting notes exist.
- [ ] Requesting structures with insufficient or contradictory notes yields the exact unresolved area and one neutral follow-up question.
- [ ] A successful request returns no more than three Article Shapes grounded exclusively in current Session Notes.
- [ ] Each Shape presents ordered section purposes, referenced notes, organizing logic, and a concise tradeoff.
- [ ] Shapes contain no invented claim, example, evidence, polished heading, or Article prose.
- [ ] The Writer can select a Shape, reorder sections, move or remove notes, and rename section-purpose labels.
- [ ] Regenerating Shapes uses the current Writer-edited note set and occurs only on explicit request.
- [ ] The Article remains unchanged through generation and Shape editing.
- [ ] Tests cover premature requests, contradictions, maximum counts, note grounding, authorship boundaries, and visible Shape editing.
