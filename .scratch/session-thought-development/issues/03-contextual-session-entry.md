# 03 — Launch Thought Development from Article context

**What to build:** Let a Writer begin the same temporary Thought-Development experience from a selected passage, Suggestion, or Priority while keeping the session topic visible and under Writer control.

**Blocked by:** 01 — Run a safe temporary Thought-Development Session.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] “Develop your ideas” remains available as the persistent general entry point.
- [ ] A Writer can launch from a selected Article passage without changing that passage.
- [ ] A Writer can launch from a Suggestion or Priority through a contextual action.
- [ ] The setup step visibly identifies and prefills the contextual source.
- [ ] The Writer can edit the proposed topic before the first Coach question.
- [ ] Context is used only to focus questions and is never converted into invented Session Notes.
- [ ] Stale or unavailable source context is detected before questioning begins and explained without opening a corrupted Session.
- [ ] Tests cover all four entry paths and verify that the Article and coaching artifacts remain unchanged.
