# Draft snapshot calibration

Type: prototype
Status: resolved
Assignee: Codex

## Question

What observable anchors distinguish scores across clarity, structure, concision, correctness, audience fit, and voice consistency, and how should an early or intentionally unconventional draft communicate uncertainty?

## Comments

### Resolution — 2026-09-12

Use a shared five-point scale based on observable reader impact, with category-specific evidence for clarity, structure, concision, correctness, audience fit, and voice consistency. Keep confidence separate from score: show “not enough evidence” instead of a low score when the draft is too early, and withhold the overall score until at least four categories are assessable. Treat an unconventional pattern as a reason to ask about intent and lower confidence—not as a penalty; once the Writer explains the choice, judge whether it achieves its intended effect while retaining any observable reader friction.

Validated by the interactive early-sketch, maturing-draft, and intentional-fragments cases.
