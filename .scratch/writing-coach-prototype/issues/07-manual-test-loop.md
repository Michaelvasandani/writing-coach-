## Question

What exact tester tasks, observations, interview questions, and stop-or-iterate signals will determine whether the integrated prototype makes writing stronger while preserving ownership and voice?

## Comments

### Resolution — 2026-09-12

Keep testing deliberately informal. Writers explore the integrated prototype freely, without scripted tasks or facilitator-directed coverage, and report problems to the builder in person. The builder observes whatever occurs naturally, decides which feedback is actionable, and iterates manually.

Do not add a formal interview script, scoring rubric, comparison condition, dedicated test instrumentation, or numerical stop threshold for this prototype effort. Testing is a lightweight source of product judgment, not evidence of efficacy. The builder decides when feedback warrants another iteration and when the prototype is good enough to conclude the effort.
