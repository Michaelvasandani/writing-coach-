When does a Draft Snapshot know enough to speak?

This demo tests whether a five-point, evidence-anchored model can distinguish six coaching qualities without turning an early draft into a bad grade or treating an intentional convention-break as an error.

—overall

Draft Snapshot

Shared five-point anchor

1 · BlockingThe pattern repeatedly prevents the intended reading.
2 · StrainingRepeated friction makes the reader work too hard.
3 · WorkingThe draft generally works, with a consequential weakness.
4 · StrongThe quality supports the article, with only local friction.
5 · DistinctiveThe quality is consistently effective for this intent.
  • A score describes observed reader impact, not effort, talent, or draft completeness.
  • “Not enough evidence” is different from a low score.
  • The Coach names the observed pattern behind every score.
  • Unconventional is not a penalty; unexplained intent lowers confidence, not the score by itself.

Decision this prototype is testing

Use one shared 1–5 impact scale, category-specific observable evidence, and a separate confidence state. Delay structure and voice scores until enough text exists. Let stated intent reinterpret a convention-break, but never erase observable reader friction.
